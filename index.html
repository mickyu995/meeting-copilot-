    When does this work?

    The phone's microphone must hear the other person. It works well for in-person meetings, and for a call that plays on another device (laptop, tablet) placed near the phone. If the call is on this same phone, Android often gives the microphone to the call app and this page may hear nothing.

    Keep the screen on and this page open. Chrome on Android gives the best result.

    LISTENING AND ANSWERS
    PRIVACY

    Briefs, transcripts and notes stay on this phone. While you listen, Chrome sends the microphone audio to Google's speech service, and each answer sends your brief and the recent conversation to Gemini with your key. On a free Gemini key, Google may use that content to improve its products, so use a paid key for sensitive meetings.

    Use it responsibly. In many places you must tell people before you transcribe a call, and some workplaces, schools and exams do not allow assistants like this.
